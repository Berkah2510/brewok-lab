# news-image-20261123 — "The World Ledger"

Entry for make.fun competition news-image-20261123 (23 November 2026 world news in one image).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) draws the 1024x1024 `entry.png` newsprint front page —
masthead with the date, ten story panels covering 23 Nov 2026 events (Dymer mourning,
Poland missile confirmation, Benghazi drone-boat sinking, COP31 collapse, Iran blockade,
Zelenskyy in Berlin, US House 258, Delhi aid for Nepal, Khartoum drone strikes, Seoul AI
exam scandal) plus a World-in-Brief strip. Fully deterministic; `python3 main.py --verify`
checks the PNG.
